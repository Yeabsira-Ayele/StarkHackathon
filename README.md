# LEWEGENE 🇪🇹

### Together, We Make the Heavy Lighter.

Lewegene is a digital fundraising platform designed to connect people who need support with people willing to help. Built with Ethiopian communities in mind, it enables individuals and organizations to raise funds for medical care, education, emergencies, community development, and other meaningful causes.

Our guiding idea is simple: **a burden that is too heavy for one person becomes easier when many people contribute together.**

Imagine one person carrying 50 lemons. The load may be difficult for one person, but when 50 people each contribute one lemon, the burden is shared. Lewegene brings this spirit of collective support into the digital world.

## The Problem

Individuals, families, and community organizations can struggle to raise the money needed for urgent and important causes. Connecting with potential supporters, reaching people beyond one's immediate community, and coordinating contributions can be challenging.

## Our Solution

Lewegene provides a digital space where fundraisers can present their causes, supporters can discover campaigns and contribute, and organizations can seek support for their initiatives.

The platform aims to make fundraising more accessible, connect communities, and encourage collective action.

## Key Features

* **Campaign Fundraising:** Create and manage fundraising campaigns for different causes.
* **Discover Causes:** Explore campaigns and find opportunities to support others.
* **Donations:** Support campaigns through the available donation workflow.
* **Organization Profiles:** Enable organizations to present their identity and initiatives.
* **Reporting:** Allow users to report concerns through the platform's reporting functionality.
* **Administration:** Support platform oversight and campaign review.
* **Multilingual Experience:** Support accessibility for Ethiopian users through language options.
* **Voxide:** Our voice-oriented initiative aimed at making fundraising more accessible through voice interaction.

*Feature availability and functionality should be verified against the deployed version.*

## Technology Stack

Lewegene is built using modern web technologies.

* **Frontend:** React, TypeScript, Vite, and Tailwind CSS.
* **Backend:** Node.js and Express.
* **Database:** MongoDB.
* **Deployment:** The project's configured hosting services.

## How to Explore Lewegene

1. Open the deployed website.
2. Explore the home page and discover available campaigns.
3. Open a campaign to understand its purpose and fundraising goal.
4. Explore the available donation process.
5. Review registration, user profiles, and organization features.
6. Explore other available features, including reporting and administration, using authorized test accounts where necessary.

Please use test accounts and test data when evaluating workflows that could create real transactions or modify live records.

## Our Vision

We believe meaningful change does not always begin with a large contribution. It can begin with many people choosing to do a little.

Lewegene aims to make that collective effort easier by connecting people, supporting communities, and turning shared compassion into action.
